# Riding Shotgun pt. 6

Spoiler: Winning vote

- [x] Time for some science at Warehouse-kun
- [x] Grief release experiment.

You hum for a moment, thinking over Mami's question. You *could* go check up on the various friends you've made around Mitakihara, the ones you don't see that often, but there *is* a little quirk of thaumodynamics that's been on your mind. Now might be a good time to test it, you think.

You feel a grin stretching your cheeks.

"I think..." you say. You'd rub your hands together, but you have Mami leaning against you, and you don't want to disturb her, so you'll settle for a cackle that's only mildly maniacal. "I think I'm going to do some *Science*."

Mami giggles, finally straightening up and planting a gentle kiss on your cheek.

"Well... do be careful, alright?" she says, the lightness of her tone belying the way golden eyes fix on yours with a very genuine concern.

"I will," you promise, taking her hands and squeezing gently. "I... actually, two things. First - Sayaka, can I borrow a Sayaka to keep an eye out? I can't promise it'll be interesting, and frankly if things are interesting that means something went *wrong*, but..."

"Sure," Sayaka says, giving you a *Look*, which you take to mean that she's ignoring your phrasing. She holds up her key-ring of power charms. "We've got math first thing after lunch, so I'll take anything to keep me awake. Want me to bring any particular powers?"

"Math *right* after eating?" you gasp, pressing a hand to your heart. "Oh no, I can feel my opinion of Mitakihara Middle School's educational standards plummeting."

"They can't *not* have a class after lunch, after all," Madoka says reasonably, drumming her fingers on her empty lunchbox. "Though maybe... art would be a better choice? It's, ah, a little less intensive on analytical thinking."

Hitomi snickers as she rises to her feet, dusting her skirt off.

"Mitakihara Middle School *is* still in the top hundred schools in the nation, you know," she says. "*Regardless* of unfortunate post-prandial scheduling."

"Top hundred... so that makes it top one percent?" you muse. "Didn't know that, neat. But anyway. Sidetrack aside..."

You bite your lip, turning to Homura and trying to suppress the guilty squirm in your stomach.

"I... Homura, this is that one experiment I alluded to a while ago, that I was asking your help for?" you say. "When we were hunting for Sasa."

You *could* have just kept quiet about this - chances are Homura forgot, anyway. You *could* have just asked Homura for a word under the aegis of timestop. But neither feels right, not when these are your *friends*. You're involving Sayaka in this directly, Mami deserves to know. And Madoka and Hitomi aren't mushrooms to be kept in the dark, they're not lesser because they're not magical girls.

Homura frowns slightly, then nods, her look turning to faint concern.

"Yeah," you say, and smile as reassuringly as you can. "I'm... I hope it's alright with you? This *should not* be a very dangerous experiment, and Sayaka's antimagic should be just as good a backstop for this experiment, in case anything goes wrong."

"... alright, now *I'm* having a bit of doubt about this whole thing," Sayaka says, scowling and propping her hands on her hips. "I'm sure antimagic's gonna do plenty, but if... well, no. If anything *does* go wrong, I'd know, and Homura can grab the actual me and Mami and be over to you in a moment."

And now you're getting hit with concerned looks from *everyone* - thoughtful and evaluating from Mami and Homura, quiet worry from Hitomi, and maybe worst of all, disappointment from Madoka.

"It *really shouldn't* be that dangerous, just potentially volatile," you say. "It's... it's no worse than chem lab in school or something - I might be working with dangerous stuff, but I'm making sure to take proper safety precautions for it, and to have someone keeping an eye out. And this *is* an important experiment to actually do."

Mami sighs, taking your hand in both of yours and squeezing gently.

"We'll hold you to it," she says softly. "Be *careful*, Sabrina."

"I promise I will," you say, meeting her eyes solemnly, and squeezing her hand gently before turning to look everyone else in the eye, trying to impress your sincerity upon them. "And I'll update everyone after the experiments wrap up, to make sure everyone knows I'm safe?"

"You better!" Madoka says, returning your look with ferocious concern. "Your safety is important, Sabrina!"

"I know. I will," you say. You're tempted to wave it off, but... not in the face of earnest concern from your friends. You turn your attention last to Homura, as the one you'd made the initial request of, and finally, she gives you a shallow nod.

"If anything happens, tell me," she murmurs. "I'll be there."

"I will," you say. "Thank you."

"... yeah, alright," Sayaka says, transforming in a blaze of light. "I suppose someone *does* have to be your minder. Gimme a sec to make a clone, and then we gotta go for class. Bell's in a couple of minutes."

You don't miss the way Homura's eyes flicker to Sayaka at the flash of cerulean blue, then to Madoka. But then, neither do you miss the way Madoka's expression twitches towards *concern* as she glances at Sayaka's Soul Gem, then to your face.

"Yeah, sorry for keeping you," you say, deciding not to comment on that. Instead, you offer Homura a quick, reassuring smile, and snap your fingers to draw attention to yourself instead. "And, uh. Right. Getting back to the point. Antimagic's the only important one, I think, but feel free to bring anything else along?"

"Antimagic," Sayaka says, thumbing through the multi-coloured jewels. "I'll just... toss in a grabbag of other stuff. Maybe Ueda's power too."

"Sounds good," you say, nodding as you turn back to Mami. "I'll be careful, OK? And I'll have Sayaka to watch my back, so I'll be fine."

Mami smiles at you, brilliant and dazzling.

"Thank you," she says. "And Sayaka, you take care of Sabrina, alright? We all know she can be reckless."

"Yeah, yeah," Sayaka says. There's two of her now, her clone in magical girl costume while the real Sayaka's back in her school uniform, but *both* of her roll her eyes. "I'll bring your girl back safe."

"That will be much appreciated," Mami says gravely despite the pink dusting her cheeks.

"I'm not *that* bad," you say, trying not to blush yourself. "Anyway, *anyway*, I've kept you all for too long. See you all after school!"

"Yup!" Madoka says happily, already hovering at the exit to the stairwell. "See you!"

You exchange your goodbyes, sharing a lingering hug with Mami before she leaves, Sayaka giving you a tolerant, amused smirk as you turn away from watching her disappear down the stairs.

"You are *such* a sap," she informs you.

"Sappier than a maple forest in spring," you say shamelessly. "And besides, would *you* be any less of a sap if it was with Mami?"

"Of course I wouldn't be, suave and cool suits me much better, don't you think?" Sayaka says, snickering. "But don't worry, I like Kyousuke. Mami's not really my type."

"What do you mean, not your type?" you say, faking offense and a scowl. "Mami is *perfect*."

"Mami *is* very pretty, yes," Sayaka says, rolling her eyes. "But she's- she's just not my type, OK? I don't think of anyone else like that. I like Kyousuke."

You arch an eyebrow at her.

"I *do*. But we should get going," Sayaka says, flushing and striding to the edge of the roof. "They do check up here after the-" She winces as the school bell rings out, piercing tone singing out from the school building below. "-after the bell goes. C'mon."

You're... not going to poke at that, though if you're honest, you're not sure she's really spoken about Kyousuke recently, or even visited him, has she? Admittedly, your conversations don't usually run that way, so maybe she's been talking about him to Madoka or Hitomi, but then again, she *has* had other things occupying her time. Not much time to go visit him, either.

"Yeah, let's go," you say, joining her. You'll offer her mercy, you suppose. "And thanks for coming along."

"Like I said, you're practically doing me a favour, giving me something to keep me awake," she says, shrugging and trying to hide her fluster. "Don't sweat it."

She kicks off the edge of the roof, shimmering blue lines igniting beneath her feet as she soars off into the sky. You catch up to her in a few moments, the nightmare purple at your back driving you upwards in great, booming wingbeats to contrast the elegant waves of her cape.

"*So, previous topic but more seriously, some things just bring joy when *shared* with others who enjoy them, you know?*" You tell Sayaka as you draw level with her, telepathy reaching out effortlessly. "*Like... imagine some parallel world where I was dating *Homura*. I really don't think I'd be nearly as sappy, since it's not something she'd enjoy.*"

"*So what you're saying is that you and Mami are colossal dorks,*" Sayaka says, giving you a sidelong look. "*No, more like you *revel* in being colossal dorks.*"

"*Yup!*" you say cheerfully.

Sayaka shakes her head, trying to brush wind-mussed hair out of her eyes.

"*Ugh,*" she says. "*And... you know, I can almost see you and Homura, in a different world, you know her practically as well as you do Mami, and the way you're so protective of them both. But I also sort of can't. You're like family with Homura, somehow.*" She pauses. "*Also, she likes Madoka, right? It's not just me?*"

"*Oh, she definitely likes Madoka,*" you say carefully. "*But more than that isn't mine to talk about, you know? And I'll ask that you don't push it too much? She has... mm. She has hang-ups that deserve to be treated carefully.*"

"*'course,*" Sayaka says, snorting. "*That much I know. But that's also what I mean, you know? That sort of protectiveness.*"

"*I suppose,*" you say, shrugging. There's... entirely too much that you *can't* tell Sayaka about, that would explain why Homura deserves it. "*I mean, I'd do the same for you, or Madoka, or Hitomi, or... any of our friends who need it. I care, you know?*"

"*Yeah, you dork,*" Sayaka says. She grins at you, sharp and cheerful. "*You're a dork, but you're a damn good person.*"

"*... thanks,*" you say, ducking your head in embarrassment.

The conversation lapses after that, falling into a comfortable silence as the two of you make your way east towards the old industrial district. The river glitters beneath the afternoon sun, ships and barges motoring along with white wakes spreading wide behind them: no longer carrying the lifeblood of manufacturing, but an endless inflow of finished goods instead.

You make it to your destination without further fanfare, gliding to a gentle landing on the deserted courtyard and letting your wings dissolve. Sayaka's boots crunch on the sand behind you, scraping across the flagstones as she slides to a halt.

"Hello again, Warehouse-kun," you say cheerfully, beaming at the rusty old building. "Feels like it's been forever."

You can *feel* the force of Sayaka's eye-roll without even looking at her.

"*So,*" she says loudly. "What're we experimenting with today?"

"The big question I want to figure out today is - what happens when Grief is released from *all* control or Soul Gems?" you say, turning to face her. "I tried releasing a *little* before but didn't pursue it any further at the time."

"Huh," Sayaka says, combing her hands through her hair and settling it after the flight. "I guess I can see why that'd be important to know. What happened the last time you tried it?"

"Well, it was just a bit of Grief, but what I noticed was that the stuff tried to clump together when I released it," you say, resisting the urge to try and tame your own hair - by this point, you know it's a futile effort. "So I'm worried that there's some kind of unseen structure or some*thing* might form out of Grief, if I just let the stuff go."

"Yeeeeesh, alright," Sayaka says. She scowls, tapping her foot against the ground, and the concrete flows upwards at her gesture to form a stool which she plonks herself on. Catching your look, she shrugs. "Figured I'd get some practice with the less-used powers I copied. And it's not like you need me to *stand* and watch you do all the stuff, yeah?"

"Yeah, that's not a problem," you say. That would be Inoue Yuuna's magic, if you recall correctly - Inoue *Yuuki's* sister, both in Ishinomaki. "Anyway. Yeah. That's my main experiment for now, and I wanted someone to watch my back in case I lose awareness or something while doing it, and in theory, if something somehow *beyond* my ability to control manifests, you *should* be able to just blap it with antimagic. I... consider that to be a very low probability, but it's important to be careful."

"Cool," Sayaka says, offering you a thumbsup. "I can definitely do that. Also, why now, anyway?"

"Well, the experiment's been on my mind for a bit," you say, ticking reasons off your fingers. "I was also considering hunting more Witches with Kyouko, but I think I've probably worn out her patience for today. And given, uh... given what I found out this morning, it's probably important to know."

"What you found out this morning?" Sayaka prompts.

"... right, you wouldn't know," you say, frowning. "So. This morning, I went to visit Miss Watanabe and Miss Saito, and check in on how things are working out with the Soujus. And... OK, I'll back it up further: you know the Soujus were, ah, collecting Soul Gems, yeah?"

"Yeah?" she says, not quite hiding a grimace, then evidently realizing you'd noticed. She waves a hand at you, scowling. "Go on, go on, I'm fine."

"Uh. If you're sure?" you say, giving her a concerned look. She... probably recalls how that fight *ended*, with you rather energetically dismantling the Soujus' body.

"I'm *sure*," Sayaka grumbles. "Talked to Homura about it, even. I'm dealing, just get on with it."

"Well... alright. So I was following up on how they were keeping those Soul Gems cleansed," you say slowly. "Because that's a *lot* of Gems to support at once. And yeah, they were all unconscious, but still - and they were roaming and fighting, too. It's just... the logistics are a little weird, yeah?"

Sayaka snorts roughly.

"'Logistics wins wars'," she quotes. "Yeah. They... does that mean *they* can also cleanse Soul Gems?" Her eyes narrow. "And... wait, so. From context, what they can do somehow releases Grief into the air. That's why you want to check, because you need to follow up on that and make sure it's safe."

"Yeah. Yeah, exactly!" you say. You're not trying to devalue their technique or anything, after all, you're just trying to make sure that it *is* safe to use and to teach. "There has to be a reason that something like that isn't commonplace... well, no. There's lots of reasons that that technique isn't known, beginning from the fact that it took, ah, a very *specific* mindset to figure out, and that it's *slow*."

And that there are certain external parties that are *very interested* in making sure that mass cleansing isn't available, but you're not saying *that* out loud.

"I gotcha," Sayaka says, nodding. "It's still weird, though... I mean, it took your Wish, and it's supposed to be *impossible*, isn't it? And others who made similar Wishes don't have true cleansing either. *And* heck, I can't even copy your magic, and I have *copy magic*."

"Well, two things," you say. "First of all, there's a certain some*thing* that benefits greatly from it being considered impossible and also happens to be the primary source of communication, outliers like Miss Benouna aside. I mean, it *does* seem to be a difficult thing to achieve, but *impossible*? We're *magical girls*. Impossible is our stock-in-trade."

Sayaka snorts, then looks thoughtful.

"You think it would do that? Modify what we say?" she asks.

"Maybe," you say. You find yourself pacing a little, scuffing your boots on the sandy flagstones. "I... don't know. It wouldn't fit with it's 'oh I only tell the truth' bullshit. But it's like... if I ask about other Grief controllers, and all I hear about are horror stories, well... it doesn't need to *lie* or distort words, right?"

"Media control," Sayaka says with a nod. "Yeah. What was the second thing?"

"Second...oh. *Well*," you say, and throw your hands in the air, aggrieved. "*Right*? It's so weird! That was my *Wish*, I wished to control Grief. And don't get me wrong, I'm damn happy with my Wish. It's emblematic of what I want to do and it's given me so many ways to help people. It's *so weird* to run into another way of doing it."

Sayaka snorts.

"Yeah, I can only imagine," she says. "I suppose that's what Mami means, when she says your Wish was an audacious one."

"I suppose," you say, shrugging and shaking your head. The silence stretches out for a moment, and you take a deep breath, your steps stilling as you turn to face Sayaka. "Anyway. Enough delays, I guess. Time for science, I think - you good for it?"

"Yup," Sayaka says, sitting up straighter, hand held out to the side.

"Alright. I'll be releasing the Grief straight up above us, at the limit of my range," you say. "If anything forms, it'll have to travel down a hundred meters before affecting anyone. If I zone out, then wake me up, with antimagic if necessary, if anything does form, let me try and handle it before hitting it with antimagic. It shouldn't come to any of that, but it's important to know, and it's important to be sure. Got it?"

"Got it," Sayaka says, her attention on you.

"First test," you say, taking a deep breath and pulling a marble-sized blob of Grief from storage, balanced on the tip of your finger. "Starting with a small amount of Grief. Beginning... now."

You nod, and cast the Grief upwards with a flick of your hand.

You track it upwards, tracking it with your magical senses. You can feel Sayaka doing the same, her attention focused on the Grief as it soars upwards, eyes squinted against the noonday sun. Nothing happens, of course, even as the Grief bumps up against the edge of your range - just an inert, compressed blob of Grief, crushed by your will into a small sphere. There's no strain to it, just an awareness that you're at the limit of your reach, in the same way you can put your arm out and know that you can't stretch any further.

You brace yourself, and with a sharp twist of your mind, you shove the Grief out past your reach.

The sphere of Grief unravels instantly. Absent your will, solid Grief sublimates instantly, exploding into a billowing cloud of Grief. And yet, at the center of the mass, you can feel some of it holding together, a coalescing core before it thins out.

No, not quite.

Maybe it's that you're paying closer attention this time, maybe it's that you sort of know what to look for. Maybe it's just that you're more experienced, more settled into your senses now. Either way...

You're not sure 'coalescing' is the right word. It feels like it's *settling* somehow, almost like it's seeping into reality itself, though you're not sure either is really an accurate description of what's happening. You might compare it to water sinking into the ground instead of flowing away, or of sauce congealing on a plate as you try to rinse it away - two processes at odds with each other, depth versus breadth.

Still, nothing too different from your previous attempts, thinking back: you simply have a better eye for it now, more practice with your magical senses granting you more accurate observations.

"Right... OK. That's the same as the last time I tested that. It'll do for a baseline," you say, frowning slightly as you lower your hand and look down at Sayaka. "Anything you noticed there?"

"Not really," Sayaka says, blinking a few times. "Just the usual feel of like... Grief has a feel, you know? I can tell when it's *your* Grief, and I'm used to that, but once you actually released it, it was... ugh. *That* bit I noticed very clearly."

She grimaces.

"Also, I can stare at the sun, and it's a 'lil bright, but I'm not blind," she adds.

"... *Sayaka*," you say.

She points at herself.

"Clone," she says. "Also, healing."

You open your mouth to argue the point, but... you know, you honestly can't argue with that logic. Clones *are* excellent for testing this sort of thing, and healing *is* a good safety net that both of you can bring out. *And* as magical girls you can reasonably expect to be resistant to this sort of thing.

"... yeah, alright," you concede, and shake your head. "OK! Test two. Next test - equivalent amount of Grief, *not* compressed. So in other words, what I'd consider raw Grief, in the same state that I'd get out of a Soul Gem."

Sayaka flashes you a thumbs-up and a grin, and you take that as a sign to begin. It takes just a thought to draw enough Grief from your hammerspace and let it unspool into a billowing cloud of rippling, nightmare purple, a faint press upon your awareness. And from the way Sayaka sways back slightly, it's definitely noticeable to her too - but she simply catches your eye and grins again.

You nod, and launch the cloud of Grief upwards with a sharp gesture. Once more, you track it upwards with a careful eye, both magical and physical, and watch as it hits the edge of your range. A sharp mental shove pushes it over once more, and you watch it simply... dissipate. The settling, the seepage, whatever you want to call it, is almost imperceptible in face of the Grief dispersing into the air.

"So it's a density thing?" you murmur to yourself. "But I think I gotta repeat it."

"Oh, *responsible* science," Sayaka says, snickering. "Boring, but after that big promise to be safe, *good*. Also, uh... real quick, substitution for integration of-square-root-ofoneminusxsquared? X-to-sin-u?"

"Yup," you say. "Didn't we go over this something like last week?"

"Got put on the spot in class and wanted to be sure," Sayaka says, shrugging unabashedly.

You snort.

"Can't argue with that," you say. "Alright. A couple of repeats in different orders, and I'll, uh... try it on *that* end of the yard and on *that* end, just to be sure there isn't some kind of saturation effect happening."

You repeat the experiment a couple of times, watching as grief soars up into the skies and releasing it with an effort of will. To your satisfaction, the result remains the same, sufficiently so that you're willing to consider it your hypothesis: raw Grief dissipates fast enough that it doesn't seem to settle properly, and denser quantities of Grief seems to *seep* into the world in some indefinable way before dissipating fully.

Then again, it might be unsurprising that the results didn't change. Magic is susceptible to *people*, after all, who you are, what you think and what you expect and... OK, you're pretty sure you can think yourself into expecting a different result from the first one, but you weren't trying to. But this *is* the desired result, you suppose?

You convey as much to Sayaka, who's been looking vaguely bored as the experiments wear on, fiddling with bits of rock drawn from the ground even as she keeps that nauseating grease of antimagic ready and banked. It's been kind of a relief to have her here, you admit, to have her here maintaining her vigilant watch.

"I mean, it's good that it's being consistent?" she offers. "So it means that the Soujus' cleansing trick is fine, then?"

"I... think so," you concede reluctantly, ignoring the twinge of sour annoyance at the reminder. "I'm concerned with the seepage thing, I don't think it bodes well, but the rate at which they release Grief definitely falls below that threshold. Unless someone does it persistently in a single spot for a real long time."

Sayaka snorts.

"And there ain't that much Grief in one Soul Gem," she says. "Which reminds me, gimme a sec to cleanse before we continue?"

"Oh, yeah," you say, starting to reach out with your magic, then blinking as you're rather jarringly reminded by the lack of a Soul Gem that *this* Sayaka's just a clone. "... right, yeah."

Sayaka eyes you, then snickers.

"Don't worry about it, I always keep that Clear Seed with me," she says.

"Good," you say. "It does still worry me that you're dependent on having that, but..."

You trail off, thoughts invading your mind.

Magic is susceptible to who you are and what you think. And... Sayaka's matured a lot from those early days, hasn't she? When she made her Wish out of insecurity, out of that impulsive, burning urge to *do* something that you rather keenly *get*. She's been working alongside you as a peer and a friend, and... she *knows* more now. She knows about Grief, about Witches, and how they interact with each other.

"... hey, Sayaka?" you say. "Wanna test something?"

"Bored of your own experiments already?" she snarks, flicking a pebble at you.

"No, I'll be getting back to that soon, but I had an idea for you?" you say, letting it bounce off your coat.

"Hit me," she says. "I'm only not falling asleep in class 'cuz I've got a baby to babysit"

"Hey, I'm *at least* a toddler," you argue. Still, you offer her your hand. "Try copying my magic again. Wait, can you copy magic through your clone?"

"It didn't work last time, remember?" Sayaka says. "Copying your magic, I mean."

"Yeah, I remember. 404 error, right?" you say, quoting what she'd told you last time. "What do you do when a webpage doesn't load?"

"Refres- oh come on, magic doesn't work like that," Sayaka complains, rolling her eyes.

"It can if you believe it enough," you say seriously, and grin. You shake your hand insistently at her. "No, really. Trust me - I do think it might be different this time. What's the harm? Wasting a couple seconds and a few drops of Grief? Well, if your magic works through your clones, anyway."

"Yeah, yeah," Sayaka grumbles, clasping your hand in hers, glove on skin. "And yeah, I figured it out. Now, let's see..."

You feel her magic reach out, the cerulean fire of her magic unfurling and winding down her arm and up yours, reaching *deeper* in some indefinable way - and you feel her make *contact*, a faint, electric sensation somewhere deep within your very being, an energetic high-five, a cleanly struck musical chord, something vibrant and so very *Sayaka* radiating from your chest for a moment. Judging from the way Sayaka's eyes widen and her breath stutters, she felt the sensation, too.

"*Whoa*," she says, dropping your hand as if burned. "What th- what was *that*?"

"Heck if I know," you say, blinking even as your stomach does somersaults. That did *something*. "Copying magic from others wasn't even like that, was it?"

"Hell no," Sayaka says, shaking her head. "Mostly I just get a..." She waves her hand vaguely, trying to describe something that human language never evolved for. "*Sense* of whatever I just picked up. Wait, I-"

She puts her hand out towards you, frowning.

"I got... *something?*" she says. "I don't- huh?"

You find yourself concentrating, trying to feel any change in the Grief around you, but there's nothing at all, not even the faintest tug. In fact-

"My magic didn't quite come with a manual, but you remember my first ever application, don't you?" you say, grinning nostalgically even as you ignore the squirm of nervous anticipation deep inside you. With a ceremonial flourish, you pull your Soul Gem off your coat, and hold it out to Sayaka. "Here."

"... you..." Sayaka frown deepens, then she narrows her eyes at you. "Oh my god, you *dork*. *Mami*."

"In my defense," you begin, and look away, blushing. "Shut up." You brandish your Soul Gem at her. "Just try it. Try and take the Grief in my Soul Gem."

Sayaka rolls her eyes.

"Yeah, yeah. This is why you're not cool, you know," she says, extending her hand towards your Soul Gem as her frown returns. "*None* of the powers I get come with a manual either, but using them is like... moving your fingers or something. There's... feedback and instincts, and your magic has that too. But. Your magic... is telling me this doesn't work?"

"Well..." you say, ignoring the faint twist of relief in your chest as you check your own awareness of your magic, making sure you're relaxed, that you aren't deliberately holding on to the Grief in your Soul Gem. "It's not that I'm contesting you, or anything."

"Yeah, I kno-" Her face twitches, surprise and confusion settling onto her expression, and her next words come out as an awed whisper. "... I *can* cleanse myself."

"I-"

Sayaka can cleanse herself.

And you are *delighted*.

Bubbling joy and relief and laughter rises from deep within you to spill out into the world, unstoppable and *why would you?* as you yank Sayaka into a hug that lifts her from the ground. Because it had always been a concern, from the very beginning: that Sayaka was dependent on having constant cleansing, *far* more than any other magical girl. That she was *dependent* on you.

And sure, you'd never minded. You *want* to be reliable, you're happy she can be part of the team.

But you're far happier that she can be independent.

And there...

"Alright, alright, put me down," Sayaka grumbles - but she says it with an answering grin on her face, her arms unwinding from around your shoulders, so you'll take that as a win.

You find yourself grinning from ear to ear as you set her down, pushing down that ember smouldering in your breast. You have a name for that ember now: *jealousy*, ugly and burning, *irrational* emotion. You recognise it, acknowledge it, and... you try to set it aside.

"It's not *that* big a deal," she says, trying to scowl and folding her arms. "I- I'm pretty sure I can only cleanse myself."

"Still a heck of a thing," you say. "You might be able to combo it with other powers, and we'll have to find out if you can control it as finely as I can. And even if the answers to both of those are no, it means that you aren't totally dependent on having to have a Clear Seed."

"I suppose that's true," Sayaka says, brightening, then trying to bring that scowl back. She props a hand on her hip. "Man. You're lucky Koizumi's magic gives me this sort of... mental division and I didn't start yelling in class."

"... yeah, sorry about that," you say, rubbing your nose sheepishly.

"Nah, don't be," she says. "I'm real awake now."

"What's *different* this time?" Sayaka asks. "I mean, last time I tried to copy your magic, it was like... there was nothing there. Like you don't have magic."

"I was backing a hunch, but basically..." you say. "I think the difference is knowledge. Knowledge and mindset?"

Sayaka raises her eyebrows.

"Magic is emotion and will and *people*," you say, shrugging. "If people change, if your mindset changes, if your emotional makeup changes, then so does your magic. You're a different person now. You know more. I'd like to think you know me as a person a bit better now, and you know the truth about Grief and about..." You tap your Soul Gem meaningfully. "I thought it might affect things."

"Right," Sayaka says, that delight fading from her face at the reminder. "... right."

"Sorry," you say. "Didn't mean to bring the mood down."

"Nah, it's fine," Sayaka says, flapping both hands and waving it off.

"Well... yeah. I'm seriously happy for you, Sayaka, and I'll be honest," you say. "I'm *damn* glad there's another form of cleansing that isn't the dang *Soujus'* technique."

"You *really* don't like them, huh," Sayaka says. "It's kind of weird to see, honestly."

I don't know the full story behind them, but it's one part Wish and one part just being misguided, right? Circumstances that they couldn't have helped. It's just their cleansing technique that I have issue with."

Sayaka snorts.

"Sounds like you're jealous," she says.

"I-" You open your mouth to rebut, and then close your mouth slowly. *Should* you deny it?

Sayaka stares at you, amusement fading to disbelief.

"... hang on," she says. "Is *that* what you were talking to Mami about?"

"... maybe," you admit. "And... while I'm being honest, I... you being able to copy my magic made me a little jealous, too? I mean-" you frantically hurry to continue before she can misunderstand, "I *am* happy for you, Sayaka, I truly, sincerely am. Just. Well. A *little*. And you shouldn't let it impact your actions, I'm just being honest, and I'm dealing with it."

Sayaka stares at you, then steps forward and chops you lightly on the top of your head with the edge of her hand.

"Don't pick at your scabs, Sabrina," she says.

"If you *just* figured this sort of thing out, don't go and grind your face into it until you actually feel better 'bout it," Sayaka says. "*I* know that." She hesitates, then smiles. "I appreciate you wanting to do this sort of thing for me, but c'mon, Sabrina. Don't be such a damn teenager about it."

"I'm dealing with it! I got good advice from Kyouko *and* Mami," you say. "I'm just also being honest."

"Don't pick at your scabs, dumbass," Sayaka repeats, rolling her eyes. "nyway. What's next?"
