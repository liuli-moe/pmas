# Riding Shotgun pt. 10

What do you want, indeed. You kind of want to lead with Walpurgisnacht. To many, it would be the most important thing to know, the sort of topic that looms large over every other conversation you might have - even to you, she's not something you'd dismiss out of hand, and you feel... *reasonably* confident of your chances. Leading with that would be the honest thing to do, to show your sincerity, that you're not trying to hide anything important, but at the same time, the Queen of Witches is something to loom large and colour *every* conversation you might have.

"I want to help," you say simply, leading with something that's just as overwhelming, albeit in a different direction. "I... can truly cleanse Soul Gems at no cost, and I *do* want to offer my aid, if needed. I'm hoping to network so I can get word out, and so that word can get *to* me if anyone needs it, you know? And I genuinely do want to talk shop with another Grief controller, if possible. I've never met a fellow Grief controller."

Mako exchanges a look with Karina. They'd already heard of you, you know that - they mentioned as much at one point, on the IRC. From word of mouth, from a friend of a friend of a friend in Fukushima. They might already have learned about Constellation, depending on how much that friend walked, and they might already know about what you did to the Iowa girls.

"Though..." you rub your face, wincing. "I've... also heard that things can get ugly, when two Grief controllers meet? I don't know whether that's just a stereotype or whatever, but regardless, if, ah, Miss Hatsumi? doesn't feel comfortable meeting me, or simply doesn't want to, that's completely fine. I'd take no offense."

"I see," Mako says, her voice even. Her Japanese is accented despite what you can tell is an effort to enunciate fully - what you suspect residents of, say, Mitakihara or Tokyo would call a rural accent. But really it's just rolling and pleasant to your ears. She opens her mouth, then shuts it again, hesitating as her eyes dart to the cake box held in Karina's arms, and sighs, shaking her head. "Ah, what the hell."

"Sorry," you say, smiling awkwardly.

"Er- no, no, that wasn't directed at you," Mako says, shaking her head hard enough that her orange hair goes flying. "Just... we really didn't expect all this, even if we did offer the invitation. Er. We're glad to have you visiting, Miss Vee, Miss Tomoe, Miss Miki. And we would be happy to discuss things in more depth."

"That's quite alright," Mami says, smiling reassuringly and shooting you a look. "We understand."

"Yeah..." you say, trying not to wilt. "I'm sorry if that was, er, too much. I know I can be a lot, sometimes, I'm trying to keep a handle on it?"

Sayaka swallows a snicker, evidently resisting the urge to needle you in front of strangers, but you can tell by the way her eyes dart over to you that she's *thinking* it. Loudly.

"No, no," Mako says, waving her hands. "It's not your fault, Miss Vee, not in the slightest. Um, please don't be sad?" She exchanges a half-panicked look with Karina, then takes a deep breath, straightening. "May we invite you to our clubhouse? We, ah, will have to be careful that the teachers don't see us, but that... er, hopefully won't be a problem?"

"That sounds lovely," Mami says, then offers you an amused little smile and hooking her arm through yours. "Sabrina has plenty of experience sneaking into school, after all."

"You'll have to tell us about it," Mako says, a shy smile peeking through that stiffly formal countenance as she finally starts to relax, turning to lead you off the side of the roof.

It's not a long trip across the rooftops of Kagoshima City - it's just not that *big* a city, after all, the buildings shorter and more spread out. But it's a beautiful day out, the sky clear and blue, the grassy slopes of Sakurajima cutting a breathtaking backdrop to the skyline. You soon arrive at their school, which you find is *another* affair of glass and steel and light, not as ornately elaborate as Mitakihara Middle School, and sprawling wider rather than towering, but... still, very much the same vaguely disconcerting Neo-Renaissance style. Then again, that's just how schools are.

"Rooftop!" Karina calls. Her hair, deep blue and vibrant, trails behind her from her position in the lead of your little formation, the gold-trimmed, looping patterns drawing the eye as they flow in the wind.

Some things are the same wherever you go, you think to yourself as the five of you make a neat landing - but then, it's not like you've gone out and made a survey of schools across different countries, or even across Japan. Maybe it's specifically a *city* thing, that schools have wide, open rooftops with easy access. Maybe it was even a Wish that enabled it at some point, enshrining it in popular practice to have such.

Still, you make your way down, Karina and Mako taking the lead and listening closely. You could extend your nanofog, feel the way ahead for teachers or students, but that strikes you as a bit too overt, so you simply let them guide you to a door tucked at the back of one of the corridors, one with a hand-drawn sign proudly proclaiming the 'Paranormal Investigations Club'.

"We most certainly aren't *lying* about what we do, are we?" Mako says with a brief grin when she sees your eyes lingering. "But please, come in."

The Paranormal Investigations Club room is a cramped, cozy little thing, with a dingy window and an ancient-looking computer on a desk, and a shelf and sofa take up one side of the room. A few horror movie posters hang on the walls, but one wall is entirely dominated by a corkboard on the wall with a blown-up map of the city, comprised of dozens of A4-sheets neatly stapled to the board. Scattered across the map are little pins of various colours, and you can't help but notice the way they cluster around offices and hospitals.

"Are those... Witch locations?" Sayaka ventures curiously.

"Sightings of the supernatural," Karina says primly. "It is, of course, our job to gather such rumours and investigate them."

"*Clever,*" Sayaka says, grinning.

"I'll tell Nodoka you said so," Karina says with a smile. "It was her idea. But let's not stand around outside, it's late in the day but there's still teachers and other students around."

You all pile in, Mako waving the three of you towards the sofa while she busies herself with the cakes, pulling out some paper plates to serve them up on. Karina's eyes follow her progress, watching the colourful little cakes with the kind of hunger born of nostalgia.

"Unfortunately, we don't have a fridge or a kettle here, so the only thing we can offer is mineral water, or..." Mako pokes around, pulling out a trio of bottles, and frowns. "Or warm soda, if you want?"

"Water, please," Mami says, answering for all three of you squashed together in on the sofa.

"Yeah, can't blame ya," Mako says, flashing a brief grin and cracking open a bottle. "Water, then."

"So... Miss Vee, Miss Tomoe, Miss Miki, you mentioned that you're here to network?" Karina asks cautiously, ceremoniously cutting the first slice from one of the assorted cakes you'd brought.

That's polite prompting to pick the topic back up if you've ever heard it. You exchange a quick look with Mami, then Sayaka. Mami just gives you an encouraging smile, while Sayaka just rolls her eyes, busying herself by accepting an offered slice of cake.

You can't help but notice that they've barely made any mention of their two missing members - one of whom would be Nodoka. Hatsumi Nodoka, their Grief controller. You think about what Homura had told you before: that she seemed to be regretful of something in her past, that the guilt drove her. That if you convinced her to join you, she'd convince her friends.

Not something you're inclined to do. Homura had told you, in that same conversation, that Sono Mako's magic would be useful against Walpurgisnacht, and you'll certainly try to invite her. Just... not by guilt-tripping one of their number. But it's still something that you want to keep in mind, of how it shapes their relationships, their dynamic. You can already see that right here, with how they're carefully keeping Nodoka out of sight, metaphorically and literally. Out of protectiveness, you think, because she's *one of them*, and *that* you can more than respect.

"So... there's two things that I should raise first, because they're both kind of important to know about," you say slowly, rather than any of those thoughts. "The first because... well, I know you've heard about my magic, but hearing about it isn't the same thing as seeing it. So... would you like to see a demonstration?"

"Sure?" Mako says, nodding slowly as she perches herself on the edge of the computer desk, watching you with well-hidden tension.

You turn to Mami, only to find she's already smiling back at you, a teasing little grin on her face as she reaches for her Soul Gem. She holds it out for Mako and Karina to see, her fingers curled loosely around the five-lobed gem that is *her*, with just the barest sheen of Grief visible in the honey-gold depths.

Enough to be seen, and enough to be obvious as you draw it forth with a crook of a finger, enough for it to swirl into a little bead of Grief, hovering over your palm. Enough for Mako and Karina's eyes to track it, riveted with heavy, unwavering attention as you give them an awkward smile.

They let out twinned sighs after a moment, and break off the stare to meet each others' eyes for a long moment, the silent communication stretching out for a moment. And silent it is, a storm of emotions passing entirely in the way their eyes lock, not a twitch to indicate they might be *speaking* in telepathy. Karina sags back into the computer chair, Mako trying and failing to hide a conflicted expression.

"That's a hell o' a thing," Mako mutters, her accent thickening.

"Yeah... yeah, I know," you say, taking a sip of water to hide what you're sure is a nakedly obvious attempt to hide your discomfort. "But as I said, it's something I wanted to lead with, because it's... important. I just... I want to help, and I'm not beholden to the same constraints that others are because of my powers."

"We've heard, yes," Mako mutters, half to herself, half to you. She brushes a hand through messy orange hair, clearly thinking. "But... seein's different from *hearin'*, I suppose."

"So. Yeah," you say. "That's where I'm coming from. I want to *help*, and..." You pause, then add, reluctantly, "I can *more* than afford to help, you know?"

It would be nice if people could simply believe you when you say that you're here to help, that you lived in a world where people would just help one another just because. But you don't. Here and now, it's easier, more believable to say that you want to help because you can *afford* to help, and then prove that.

Mami notices the direction of your thoughts, of course, taking your hand in hers and squeezing gently. You shoot her a grateful smile in response, and force yourself to relax, waiting for Mako and Karina to sort through their thoughts.

"I believe you," Mako says, after a long moment. "Or... I believe you want to help. But you mentioned... two things you want to tell us first?"

"Yeah..." You sigh. "The second one's not about my magic, just some circumstances, and... OK, look. If you don't mind, I'm going to tell you *why* I want to tell you before I tell you?"

"A disclaimer?" Karina asks drily, giving you a skeptical look.

"... yeah, more or less," you say, grimacing and taking another sip of water. "I want to tell you because it's the right thing to do - or rather, it would be absolutely wrong of me to spring it on you at the last minute. It's the sort of thing that I don't think is *fair* to hide, but it's also the sort of thing I want to reassure you that if you decide you just don't want anything to do with it, that's totally *fine*, and that won't affect anything else at all. No obligation or anything, just... a request to hear me out?"

Mako squints at you.

"The way you talk about it, it sounds like you're yakuza or something," she says carefully. "We'll hear you out."

"Honestly, not a bad *analogy*," you say. "But, er, no, definitely not yakuza, or anything like that. It *is* that sort of you-should-know-what-you're-getting-into thing, though." You take a breath. "Have you ever heard of Walpurgisnacht?"

"Nnnnnooo?" Mako says, glancing at Karina, who shrugs.

You blink, turning to Mami.

"It's definitely not everyday knowledge," she answers, shaking her head. "It's... one of those things that come up as the occasional stories, when magical girls actually talk to one another."

"Ah," you say, sheepish. You turn back to Mako and Karina, your attention turning back to something more serious. "Well... Walpurgisnacht is a really powerful Witch, powerful enough to exist without a Barrier, powerful enough to wreck entire cities. And it just... appears, destroys things, and vanishes."

"Right. And y' know where it's appearing next," Mako ventures, half-question, half-statement. From her expression, you suspect she knows what the answer is already.

"Yeah," you say. "Mitakihara."

"Mm," Mako says, exchanging another look with Karina, lightning-fast. "I can see why that's... yeah. Alright."

"Take your time to think about it, if you need to?" you offer. "I'm not going to demand you make any decision about Walpurgisnacht itself right now, and I want to say up front that *regardless* of what you do decide, I consider everything else to be a separate issue. If you decide to accept any offers from us, or if you decide to help us by letting us set up a branch office, that's not at all contingent on your decision about Walpurgisnacht."

"But you *do* want to recruit us for that," Karina says.

"I will protect my home and those I love," you say, meeting her eyes without hesitation. "Which means bringing everything and every*one* I can. But I *also* believe that I am here to *help*, and I will *never* coerce anyone into the fight, whether by force or by witholding aid."

Karina's the first to look away, the reddish-pink of her eyes dropping away from yours and down to the crumbs of cake on her plate.

"Understandable," she says.

Mako takes a deep breath.

"Thank you for bein' straight with us," she says. "We... we'll think about it and discuss among ourselves, and give you an answer before you leave, if that's OK? We'll hear out your offers too, but after we make that decision."

"Of course," you say, resisting the urge to jump on the obvious joke. From the way Sayaka's head twitches in your direction, you can tell the same thought crossed her mind, too, but neither of you manage to exercise your tact. "Take all the time you need, seriously. It doesn't even have to be today - our offer of assistance will always be open, and we're always happy to come back another time."

"In the meantime... you said you were wanting to meet Nodoka?" Karina asks in a careful, measured tone. "We want to have a discussion among ourselves, and... she said she would be OK sparin' a few minutes to, er, talk shop."

You beam.

"I'd love to! As I said, I've never met anyone with similar powers to me before. And..." You bite your lip, thinking. "I promise that I won't raise my magic against her? I know that there's that... reputation that we Grief controllers have."

"... we appreciate that," Karina says after a slight hesitation. "You're right, that, ah, reputation *does* exist. But there's also a reputation for Grief controllers being... nasty people, and you've been nothing but polite and honest, Miss Vee."

"Stereotypes are *sterotypes*," Mami offers with a faint smile. "Rarely accurate in anything more than broad strokes, if that, and often simply wrong in specific. I'm sure that Miss Hatsumi is a good person, if you're this willing to protect her, and Sabrina most certainly is."

Karina glances at Mako, who grimaces, and nods her head slightly.

"It's not that," Karina says with a sigh. "It's not *just* that. I reckon you're a good person. It's- we ain't gonna spill our friend's story. But Nodoka's got- just be careful, 'kay? Think 'bout the stuff she's said on the IRC."

She looks down at her empty plate again and starts to cut more slices, brow furrowed.

"'nyway," she says. "They're on their way. Nodoka and Yugito."

"... if..." You hesitate before continuing. "If you have reason to believe that there might be problems with us meeting, then there's really no need to force it at all. I'm not here with a list of demands, you know? I'm here because I want to make friends."

Karina pulls your plate from your hands with a firm grip.

"We ain't some rural bumpkins to tiptoe 'round," she says sharply as she plops down another slice of cake and thrusts it back at you. "Nodoka's scared, Miss Vee, but she still wants to meet you. I'll aks y' to respect that."

"Ah," you say. "OK."

You exchange a look with Mami, who simply smiles back at you reassuringly as she accepts her own fresh slice of cake. She believes in you, and she tells you as much with a slight tilt of her head and a widening of her smile. Sayaka, on Mami's other side, rolls her eyes at you again - not worried in the slightest about how things are going, and even a little bored.

You find your thoughts turning to Karina's choice of words there as the conversation shifts to something lighter, discussing the cake and the cafe. Apparently, Karina had been to Mitakihara with her aunt once when she was a child, and had the opportunity to eat there and had been utterly smitten ever since. Mami chimes in with baking advice and ideas for how they might try and replicate the cake on their own, and the conversation flows more smoothly from there.

Even so...

*Scared*.

Think about the things she'd said on the IRC, which isn't much, to be honest. But you remember the [words](https://forums.sufficientvelocity.com/threads/puella-magi-adfligo-systema.2538/page-6004#post-12199647) of someone skittish and nervous, of *scared* of you. Or perhaps the *idea* of you. She'd seemed to be scared of the Tokyo Council too, the [one time](https://forums.sufficientvelocity.com/threads/puella-magi-adfligo-systema.2538/page-6234#post-13233572) you'd mentioned meeting them. Scared of the idea of... an authority casting punishment upon her...

... for something she'd done with her powers. And Homura said that she was someone driven by guilt, too.

... you have to wonder what kind of circumstances and Wish might give the sort of powers Nodoka has. You know how *you* got your magic, but you're well aware that you're nowhere near a normal case.

And now that you think about it, it just might be that she wants to talk to you *because* she thinks you might be the one to call her to account. That sort of self-flagellating guilt isn't too uncommon, after all, and it's almost certainly the same guilt-turned-to-atonement that Homura mentioned.

Well, she's not going to get that from you. And speaking of, that must be her now.

You're not the only one to notice two more magical girls arriving, of course - all five of you do. Mami tenses for the barest fraction of a second before she smoothly continues the conversation, expounding on the wonders of a steam bake for enhancing the moistness of a cake. Sayaka's head twitches slightly ceiling-wards, as if trying to see where the newcomers might be.

You feel a ghost of a touch, of featherlight, timid footsteps upon the edge of your attention. A magical girl, yes, in the same way as her companion next to her, or Mami, or Sayaka, or any other magical girl: a leashed storm of magic, of will, of life and *soul* in the shape of a person. But you're aware of her, somehow *beyond* any of that. Even if you were blind, deaf, your magical senses somehow cut off... you'd know she was approaching.

Huh.

Still, not particularly important for now. You let it pass.

Karina has a notepad out, scribbling furiously as she listens, with Mako perched to read over her shoulder with amusement.

You track the two newcomers as you chime in with suggestions of your own. The duo make their way down through the school, taking the same path that Mako had led you to get to the clubroom, and finally, they stop outside the door, evidently psyching themselves up. Or maybe it's just Nodoka psyching herself up, the five of you in the room continuing the discussion about baking and politely pretending that you don't know that they're there.

The door opens, finally, to reveal two girls in the same school uniform Mako and Karina are. You can guess who's who, just from seeing them: Yugito, dirty blonde and solidly built, pushing the door open. Nodoka, mousy and lanky, with faded blue hair pulled back into a ponytail, and dark eyes locking onto you.

You hold yout tongue, letting her make the first move, and it seems that everyone else is inclined to do the same.

"... hello," Yugito says after a moment. She nudges Nodoka into the room proper with a gentle push, awkwardly swinging the door shut behind them. "I'm Hida Yugito."

"A-and I'm Nodoka! Um, Hatsumi Nodoka!" Nodoka squeaks, startled into speech. She dips into a bow, a full ninety-degree dip of her upper body. "Pleased to meet you!"

"Pleased to meet you too," you say, beaming and offering your hand. "I'm Vee Sabrina, and these are my friends, Tomoe Mami and Miki Sayaka."

"Y-yes! Nice to meet you!" Nodoka says, then winces at the repetition. "Um, sorry."

You try not to wince yourself. She's nervous, that much is obvious, huddling in on herself and trying to take up as little room as possible. Though admittedly, part of that is that this isn't a very large clubroom for seven people to be crammed into, *and* she's at that gangly stage of teenager where she probably feels uncomfortable in her own space.

"I'm not going to harm you, Miss Hatsumi," you say gently.

She swallows, and nods vigorously.

"U-um!" she says. "You wanted to talk to me?"
